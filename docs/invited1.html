Invited Speaker : Vadym Kliuchnikov

Vadym Kliuchnikov is a quantum computing researcher working on quantum circuit synthesis, compilation, and fault-tolerant quantum computing. His research includes exact and approximate Clifford+T synthesis, topological quantum compiling, stabilizer circuit verification, and resource estimates for practical quantum advantage. He has also contributed to Q# and scalable quantum programming tools, with publications in venues including Physical Review Letters, PRX Quantum, Quantum, and ACM Transactions on Quantum Computing.
