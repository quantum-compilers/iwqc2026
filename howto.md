IWQC HOWTO : A guide for organisers
===

There are two key parts to organising IWQC -- the local stuff and the
programme stuff.  They interact around *timelines*.

## Format 

IWQC is usually a two-day in-person workshop with one track and all
participants in the one room.  In 2026 there were around 90
participants; in 2025 it was nearer 60. This is a good size.

The usual format is 2 invited talks (1 each day), a poster session,
and a number of contributed talks.  There may be a social event in the
evening.  We try to leave enough time for meaningful conversation
between participants rather than cramming in the maximum number of
talks.

IWQC is intended to be a low process low cost workshop.

## 1. Decide where it is going to be

Find a volunteer who is willing to organise the workshop in their
city. This is best done at the time of the previous workshop.
Having a local organiser team is a prerequisite for everything else.

The local organiser will be handling all the money.  Therefore they
need a **bank account** which can receive payments from sponsors, buy
things from suppliers, and cover expenses for invited speakers.  The
ability to generate invoices is very helpful.  Some organisations
(usually public sector) are not very good at this kind of thing so
better start on this immediately.

A minor factor in selecting a place is colocation with another event
where members of the community will be going anyway.  In the past we
colocated with 

* IEEE Quantum Week (2026)
* Quantum Resource Estimation (2025 and 2026)
* Quantum Physics and Logic (2023)

This is nice to do, because it effectively makes the participants
travel money go further if they like the colocated event.

There is no need to coordinate with the other event, but it is nice
for smaller events to do joint advertising and/or sponsorship.
